# StreamerX 1.0.2

## What’s new

- Tonight’s Spotlight automatically pans through featured titles and loops smoothly back to the beginning.
- Swipe through Spotlight, choose a title with its dots, or pause and resume the carousel. Motion pauses away from Home and respects reduced-motion settings.
- Profile replaces the extra top-right Settings button, with OpenBackend sign-in, registration and sign-out.
- Your sign-in is stored securely on the device and restored when you reopen StreamerX.
- Automatic update checks on launch and from Settings, with release notes before downloading.
- Signed updates are downloaded and verified before Android asks you to install.
- A What's New screen keeps the current release notes available in Settings.
- Home, Movies, Shows, Anime and Live TV remain visible together, including on narrow screens.

Live TV uses a separate Watched account. Your library is saved locally. Password reset and email verification are not enabled for StreamerX accounts yet. The original local 1.0.0 preview needs this one-time installation to enable updates.
